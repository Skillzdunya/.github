# SkillzDunya

**Government and private jobs in Pakistan, updated daily.** · [skillzdunya.com](https://skillzdunya.com)

SkillzDunya lists government and private sector jobs from across Pakistan in one place.
Every government ad is checked against the official source (FPSC, PPSC, SPSC, KPPSC, BPSC, NTS,
the National Jobs Portal and newspaper ads) and shows its posts, BPS grade, qualification,
age limit, domicile, test agency and last date. Private jobs from companies, banks, NGOs and
IT firms are listed alongside.
